# Blind test fixes, round 4, 2026-11-22

Scope: only the Skeleton kit parts of the reports of sonnet4a, sonnet4b and sonnet4c, the three items the lead checked on the live site, and the lead's pictures `sk-howto.png` and `sk-wait.png`. Notes about the other apps are not part of this list.

The job needed three folders, and the kit boundary rule says one task changes one folder. The lead's three required items cannot be done in one: the landing copy lives in `site/`, the how to play text and the Space key message come from `apps/garden`, and the screen that shows whole sentences and the message for a key are in `kit/shell`. The kit change is small and general: two optional fields on `InputMap` (`howTo`, `refusedKey`). It names no word from a game. This is stated here so the lead can decide whether to split it.

No package was added. No rule, level, proof file, theme, font or token was changed. No branch, commit or deploy was made.

## Overlap claims

No tester of this app claims that something overlaps. The one sticky bar claim (sonnet4c, `a2s1.png`) is about the other app. The landing page, the how to play screen and the play screen were checked in normal windows at 360 by 640, 390 by 844 and 1920 by 1080 (the how to play screen also at 375 by 667 by the existing layout test). Nothing overlaps and every control is 16 px inside the screen.

- `kit/shell/src/screens/how.test.ts`: 1 of 3 (the sentences were not listed). `kit/shell/src/mount-refused-key.test.ts`: 1 of 3 (no note after the key). `apps/garden/src/how-to-play.test.ts`: 6 of 9 (no `howTo`, no `refusedKey`). The cases that passed first are the ones that check that nothing is shown for an unknown key or a key that works.
- `e2e/blind-test-r4.e2e.ts`: 12 of 16 failed on the old build: the three landing cases at each of the three sizes (9), the code box case, the garden how to play case, and the Space key case. The how to play size cases, the hedge case and the runner strip case passed first, as noted in the table.
- The first version of the Space key browser test passed for the wrong reason: after a click on Wait the button kept focus, and Space then pressed that button. The test was changed so that the key is pressed first with no button in focus. It then failed with "element not found" for the note, which is the fault.

- `e2e/blind-test-fixes.e2e.ts` "garden how to play: it says how to get 3 stars and how a level is lost" failed with the first text. The text now says "3 stars" and "lost". The test is unchanged.

They are about the text that the lead asked to replace, so the expected text was replaced by the new sentences, not loosened: `e2e/garden-good-path.e2e.ts` (the first five items of the how to play list) and `e2e/blind-test-r3.e2e.ts` (the screen names the key 1 to 5 and the key Space: now "press 1 to 5" and "press Space").
