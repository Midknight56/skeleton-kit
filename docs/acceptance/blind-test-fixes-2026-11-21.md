## Repairs after the gate

After the gate 2 run, full verify failed on two layout checks:
1. `e2e/look.e2e.ts` (landing page at 360x640): `.intro-signs` used `gap: 8px`, below the 12px minimum spacing between neighbouring controls. Repaired by setting `gap: 12px` in `site/index.html`.
2. `e2e/play-layout.e2e.ts` (runner play screen layout across all 7 viewports): extending `boxForAnchor` vertically caused the chip distance from its anchor to exceed the 12px limit (measured 78px to 206px). Repaired in `kit/shell/src/mount.ts` by keeping `boxForAnchor` returning the true player box and adding the vertical jump corridor above the anchor to `obstacles`. This places the chip beside the player within 4px of the anchor, keeping it clear of the candle line and satisfying the 12px layout proximity rule.
