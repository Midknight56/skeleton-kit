Privacy

The games have no account, no login and no personal data. They load no font, script or image from another site.

What the page keeps on your device

Progress and settings are kept in the localStorage of your browser. There is one key for each game:

The page does not send this data anywhere. Clear it in the settings of the game with Reset progress, or in your browser.

The usage beacon

These are all the events, and every field of each one. No user id, no time of day and no other data is sent.

Each game has a Usage data switch in its settings. While it is off, that game sends no event at all. The choice is kept on your device.
